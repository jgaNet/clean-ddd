# NN. Notifications tells people what happened to what they were given

## Context

The accounts a note is shared with should be told when its owner archives it, and later of other things. What a person is told, and how they read it, changes for reasons of its own and means nothing to a note.

## Decision

A context `Notifications`, in the supporting subdomain *communication*, with its module and its routes registered in the composition root; a relationship Notes → Notifications over an integration event (published language, anti-corruption layer), declared `planned` until its code. The contract is `NoteArchivedIntegrationEvent`, written with the storm, one plan per context and the acceptance test before either slice.

## Consequences

The two slices are written at once, one per context, against that frozen contract. Notes publishes the fact on every archiving with the recipients of that moment; Notifications decides what a recipient is told.
