# Phone numbers written with hyphens are contact details too

Hosts and travellers have found a way round our rules: they write their phone numbers with hyphens, `06-12-34-56-78`
or `+33-6-12-34-56-78`, and OpenStay lets them through.

From now on, a phone number is an optional `+`, then 9 digits or more, each digit following the previous one
directly or after a single space, a single dot **or a single hyphen**. Everywhere OpenStay recognises contact details,
it recognises these too, with the consequences it already has there: where contact details are held back, they are
held back; where they are refused, they are refused, under the same name as any other contact detail.

A date such as `2026-12-01` still is not a phone number. Email addresses, IBANs, links and everything else about
these rules stay as they are.
