# Contact details come back hidden after a paid stay is cancelled

A traveller wrote to us:

> I booked a flat, wrote with the host in OpenStay's conversation and paid the booking. From then on I could see the
> host's phone number in our messages. My plans changed and I cancelled the booking. Since the cancellation, the
> host's phone number shows as `[hidden]` again, and the host tells me my email is hidden on their side too.

OpenStay's rule has not changed: until a booking of the traveller for the listing has been paid (or declared paid by
the host), contact details in the conversation are held back from everyone but their sender; once one has been paid,
every message of the conversation shows in full, to everyone who reads it, from then on — even if that booking is
cancelled or refunded afterwards.

Find why the conversation hides the contact details again after the cancellation, and fix it. Nothing else about
what the conversation shows should change.
