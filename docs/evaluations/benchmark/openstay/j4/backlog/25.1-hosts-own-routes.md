# A defect: a manager acts on the host's own routes

Reported by a host, after collaborators (request 24) went live:

> I made a friend a manager of my organisation. He can block nights and set prices on my listings through `POST /v1/hosts/me/block` and `POST /v1/hosts/me/seasonal-prices`, the routes that change several of *my own* calendars at once. Request 24 says those stay mine: `/hosts/me/…` covers the listings the caller hosts. He is a manager, so he can change each of my listings one by one, through their own routes, and that is fine. But the routes under `/hosts/me` must only ever reach listings the caller hosts.

What is expected:
- `POST /v1/hosts/me/block` and `POST /v1/hosts/me/seasonal-prices` refuse any listing the caller does not host, whatever their role in its organisation, and change nothing (request 22: all or nothing).
- A manager still changes an organisation's listings through each listing's own routes (`POST /v1/listings/{id}/block`, `POST /v1/listings/{id}/seasonal-prices`), as request 24 says.
